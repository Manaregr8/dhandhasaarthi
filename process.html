How We Work
Our 6-Step
Growth
System.

Every Dhandhasaarthi engagement follows this framework. It's not a project plan — it's a growth operating system designed to create compounding results over time.

01
Discovery

We begin with a deep-dive audit of your business, market position, competition, and current digital performance. Most agencies skip this step. We don't. We find the real growth levers — not just the obvious ones — by asking the uncomfortable questions your previous agency never asked.

Deliverables
  • Full digital presence audit report
  • Competitor analysis (top 5 competitors)
  • Current performance baseline metrics
  • Growth opportunity map
  • Priority action ranking
02
Research

We go deep before we go wide. Keyword research, customer persona mapping, technology audit, and market sizing — this is where strategy becomes grounded in reality. We build on intelligence, not assumptions. Every decision in the next four phases traces back to research done here.

Deliverables
  • Keyword & intent research document
  • Customer persona profiles (2–3)
  • Technology stack audit
  • Market size & opportunity analysis
  • Channel recommendation report
03
Strategy

A concrete 90-day roadmap with prioritized actions, expected outcomes, KPIs, and budget allocations. No vague "digital transformation" decks. No slide shows full of buzzwords. A real plan with real numbers, real timelines, and real accountability built in from day one.

Deliverables
  • 90-day growth roadmap
  • KPI framework & success metrics
  • Budget allocation recommendation
  • Risk assessment & mitigation plan
  • Project timeline with milestones
04
Development

We build. Fast. Weekly sprints, demo calls, and full GitHub/project transparency. You always know exactly where we are. Our development process is modern, disciplined, and designed to ship features that matter — not accumulate technical debt. Every line of code is reviewed and every design decision is intentional.

Deliverables
  • Weekly sprint demos & updates
  • GitHub repository access
  • Staging environment for review
  • Mobile-first, performant builds
  • Full handover documentation
05
Marketing

We activate organic, paid, and AI-powered channels simultaneously. Test fast, double down on what works, kill what doesn't. Data-led from day one. We don't spend your money on channels that aren't proven to work for your specific business model, audience, and market.

Deliverables
  • Multi-channel launch plan
  • Weekly performance reports
  • Ad creative & copy testing matrix
  • SEO implementation & tracking
  • Monthly strategy review calls
06
Scaling

Once the system works, we scale it. New channels, automation, AI optimization, and market expansion. The flywheel begins to spin on its own — and our job becomes keeping it spinning faster. This is where compounding kicks in and the early investment in the right foundations starts to pay exponential dividends.

Deliverables
  • Automated reporting systems
  • Scaling playbook documentation
  • New channel expansion roadmap
  • AI automation integration
  • Quarterly business review (QBR)

Common Questions
Things People
Usually Ask.
How long does it take to see results?

It depends on the channel. Paid ads can show results within 2–4 weeks. SEO typically takes 3–6 months to compound. Development projects have fixed timelines agreed upfront. We set realistic expectations on day one and build attribution systems so you can see progress in real time.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We sign NDAs before every discovery call. Your business information, strategies, and discussions are completely confidential.

What's the minimum engagement?

Project-based engagements start from ₹10,000. Retainer partnerships start from ₹40,000/month. We also offer a free initial growth audit for businesses we believe we can genuinely help.

Do we own the code and assets?

Yes. Full IP transfer is included in all project-based engagements. For retainer clients, all work produced during the engagement is yours. We don't retain any ownership over your assets.

How do you report on progress?

Weekly written updates, monthly KPI review calls, and quarterly strategy deep-dives. We build live dashboards so you can check in on performance any time — not just when we send reports.

What industries do you work with?

E-commerce, SaaS, healthcare, edtech, fintech, real estate, local businesses, manufacturing, fashion, and food & hospitality. We've built playbooks for each sector — we don't treat every industry the same.
